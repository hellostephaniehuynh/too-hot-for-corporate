privacy policy.

Short version: we collect your email so we can tell you when the drops are real. That's it. Here's the long version.

last updated — august 2026

who this is

too hot for corporate ("we," "us") runs this site at toohotfor.com. This policy covers what we collect through it, why, and what say you have over it. Questions go to hello@toohotfor.com.

what we collect

Just your email address, and only if you give it to us — through the movement-wide waitlist or a product-specific waitlist form. If you sign up for a specific drop, we also note which one, so we know what to email you about.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, address, or payment details anywhere on this site, because nothing is for sale yet.

how we use it

To email you when a drop goes live, when the waitlist opens for early access, or with occasional movement updates. Nothing else — no third-party marketing, no "partners you might like," no resale of any kind.

who else sees it

Two vendors process data on our behalf, strictly to run the site:

  • Formspree handles waitlist form submissions and stores the emails you submit. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts the site and, like any host, logs standard technical data (IP address, browser type, request times) for security and performance. See Netlify's privacy policy.

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone, for any reason.

cookies & tracking

This site doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers, and it doesn't set its own cookies. If that changes — say, we add analytics to see which drop people actually want — we'll update this page.

how long we keep it

We keep your email until a drop ships and you've either bought in or told us to stop, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

your say in this

Every email we send has an unsubscribe option. You can also just email hello@toohotfor.com and ask us to remove your information — no exit interview, no reason required.

kids

This site isn't directed at anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

changes to this policy

If this changes in any meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Checking back is on you — we won't email you about a policy update.

contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: hello@toohotfor.com.
